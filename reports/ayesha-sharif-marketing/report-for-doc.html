UI and image style

  • Layout: centred editorial sections, generous whitespace, responsive grids, 1,200–1,440 px content widths.
  • Cards: white or translucent “glass” surfaces; typically 8–10 px radius; soft shadows such as 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.06).
  • Buttons: compact 4–6 px radius; sage fill, forest hover, gold focus; uppercase/tracked labels in major CTAs.
  • Gradients/backgrounds: cream base with low-opacity forest, soft grey and gold gradients; literary fragments, hairlines and subtle dust.
  • Icons: consistent Lucide outline icons, generally small and functional.
  • Photography: effectively absent. Book cards generate fallback covers; there are no owned author, office, event or production photographs.
  • Desktop/mobile consistency: responsive component classes and mobile breakpoints preserve the same palette and hierarchy. The current visual identity is suited to square and vertical social templates, but dedicated templates do not exist.

7. Marketing Message Draft

Current message and positioning

Repository messages: “Quality books, delivered to your doorstep,” “Browse Books,” “Original publications for readers, students, and lifelong learners across Bangladesh,” “simple, useful, and reader-friendly books,” and “Search, filter, and order books.”

Clarity assessment:

  • What it offers: reasonably clear—an online book catalogue and ordering flow.
  • Who it serves: weak—“readers and students” appears, but audience priorities are unverified.
  • Why choose it: weak—quality, originality, convenience and delivery are asserted without proof.
  • Differentiation: not established.
  • Next action: clear—browse, add to cart, buy, checkout or contact via WhatsApp.

Tone-of-voice guide

Recommended voice: calm, clear, culturally aware, reader-first and evidence-led.

  1. Be specific: name the book, audience, format, price and availability.
  2. Be warm, not exaggerated: invite discovery without “best,” “number one” or unsupported superlatives.
  3. Respect both languages: write natural Bengali and English rather than literal translation.
  4. Make action easy: short CTAs with one clear next step.
  5. Show proof: attach ISBN, author, excerpt, review source or delivery terms whenever claimed.

Use: discover, browse, printed book, author, edition, available, pre-order, read a sample, order online, verified review.
Avoid until proven: 400+ books, bestseller, original, award-winning, nationwide, guaranteed, easy returns, free delivery, premium quality.
