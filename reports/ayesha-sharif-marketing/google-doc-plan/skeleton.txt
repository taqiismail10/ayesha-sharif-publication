7. Marketing Message Draft
Current message and positioning
Repository messages: “Quality books, delivered to your doorstep,” “Browse Books,” “Original publications for readers, students, and lifelong learners across Bangladesh,” “simple, useful, and reader-friendly books,” and “Search, filter, and order books.”
Clarity assessment:
What it offers: reasonably clear—an online book catalogue and ordering flow.
Who it serves: weak—“readers and students” appears, but audience priorities are unverified.
Why choose it: weak—quality, originality, convenience and delivery are asserted without proof.
Differentiation: not established.
Next action: clear—browse, add to cart, buy, checkout or contact via WhatsApp.

Preliminary drafts
These drafts use only repository-supported capabilities. The preferred legal name and Bangladesh positioning still require owner approval.
Short tagline: Books made easier to discover and order.
Longer tagline: A clear, reader-friendly way to browse printed books and order online.
Positioning statement: Ayesha Sharif Publication provides a direct online catalogue where readers can discover printed books, review key details and place an order through a straightforward checkout.
Hero headline: Browse books. Order online.
Hero subheading: Explore the catalogue, check availability and choose the ordering option that works for you.
Thirty-word description: Ayesha Sharif Publication offers an online catalogue for discovering printed books, viewing essential publication details and placing orders through a clear, practical checkout experience.
Fifty-word social bio: Discover printed books from Ayesha Sharif Publication through a simple online catalogue. Browse titles, check prices and availability, save books for later, or place an order through guest or account checkout. Official catalogue, author and delivery information will be added after owner verification.
One-hundred-word introduction: Ayesha Sharif Publication is developing a reader-friendly online home for discovering and ordering printed books. The website allows visitors to browse the catalogue, search by title or author, review book information, save books, add available titles to a cart and complete an order as a guest or registered customer. The current repository also supports pre-orders, manual payment options and delivery-area selection. Before public marketing begins, the publication’s official name, catalogue, authors, delivery terms, contact details and brand story should be verified by the owner so that every campaign is accurate, consistent and trustworthy.

Tone-of-voice guide
Recommended voice: calm, clear, culturally aware, reader-first and evidence-led.
Be specific: name the book, audience, format, price and availability.
Be warm, not exaggerated: invite discovery without “best,” “number one” or unsupported superlatives.
Respect both languages: write natural Bengali and English rather than literal translation.
Make action easy: short CTAs with one clear next step.
Show proof: attach ISBN, author, excerpt, review source or delivery terms whenever claimed.

Use: discover, browse, printed book, author, edition, available, pre-order, read a sample, order online, verified review. Avoid until proven: 400+ books, bestseller, original, award-winning, nationwide, guaranteed, easy returns, free delivery, premium quality.

10. Marketing Readiness Verdict
Decision
OWNER_INFORMATION_REQUIRED
The repository is sufficient to define a preliminary visual and editorial marketing direction, but it is not sufficient to launch factual book, author, trust, delivery, service or paid-advertising campaigns safely.
Recommended next steps
Approve the English/Bengali name and short form.
Replace the demo catalogue, generated ISBNs, placeholders and unsupported claims.
Verify contact, domain, social, payment, delivery and policy information.
Secure rights-cleared book covers, author portraits, excerpts and photography.
Approve positioning, priority audiences, campaign goals and proof library.
Create channel templates from the forest/sage/cream/gold system.
Launch a single verified book or brand campaign before scaling channels.

Further questions
The most decision-changing unknowns are the real catalogue, priority audience, differentiation, proof, distribution footprint and first campaign. These determine whether the visual system should lean primarily literary, educational, faith-oriented, family-oriented or broad reader retail.
Caveats and assumptions
